About Gather & Thrive

Being together should be accessible.

We know how difficult it can be to find a spacious place to gather that is quiet for people with hearing loss and accessible for people with mobility limitations.

Gather & Thrive helps people with disabilities find ways to gather and stay connected in the Missoula community.

Our current availability

We are currently limited in our ability to help with hosting. If you are interested in a gathering or volunteering, please email us or write to our post office box.

Places to come together

Gathering spaces in Missoula

We use meeting spaces at the Headwaters Foundation and large rooms at the Missoula Public Library as places to gather.

Headwaters Foundation

The space includes a full catering kitchen and two meeting rooms that can be configured to meet your needs.

Missoula Public Library

The library’s large rooms offer another place for people to gather.

Every gathering is different. Please tell us about the space and access needs that matter to you when you get in touch.

Start with what matters to you

Tell us about your gathering

You can reach out for yourself or someone you know. These details can help start the conversation.

  1. Your idea

    Who would you like to bring together, and what would you like to do?

  2. Your access needs

    Tell us what would help you participate, such as a quieter room, space to move, or a particular seating arrangement.

  3. A way to reply

    Include your preferred contact details so we can respond to your inquiry.

A few useful details

Questions about gathering

Who does Gather & Thrive help?

We help people with disabilities gather with family, friends, and community members in Missoula, Montana.

Can I ask about a gathering for someone else?

Yes. You can contact us for yourself or someone you know. Tell us about the gathering and what would help the person participate.

Are you currently helping host gatherings?

Our ability to help with hosting is currently limited. Please email us or write to us to ask about your gathering and our availability.

How can I volunteer?

Email us or write to Gather & Thrive at the post office box listed below. Let us know how you would like to help and how we can reach you.
